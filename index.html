Welcome to KnitTogether'26
The 2nd European CL Research Summit for Junior Researchers

Topic: Interpretability for Social Good

Great research arises from great collaborations. However, establishing collaborations is challenging, and it depends on the context of the institution, the supervision, existing networks, and one's personality. With KnitTogether'26, we invite NLP researchers, particularly junior ones, who work on Interpretability for Social Good, to spend an exciting week in the Swiss mountains.

In a growing community dealing with a topic gaining increasing societal influence and relevance, we want to join forces and exchange ideas. What can we learn from each other?

In particular, but not limited to, we would like to explore: a) How can interpretability methods help us understand and mitigate harmful behavior of language models? b) How can explanations make NLP systems more transparent and accountable to the people affected by them? c) Where do current interpretability methods fall short when applied to socially relevant problems? We would like to hear the different perspectives of the participants: How do you relate your work to the topic? Using innovative and playful methods, we want to learn more about the work of our peers and thus get to know each other better scientifically.

During a week full of hikes and other fun activities, the goal is to get to know each other without hurdles, draft ideas, and discuss research. We want to create a safe space where everyone can openly discuss their work and ideas without academic pressure.

Accomodation

The accommodation is essentially a Swiss mountain hut that provides shared rooms (4, 6, up to 28). It features a fully equipped kitchen, multiple common areas including a dining room and a ping-pong space, and outdoor amenities such as a terrace and a large playfield with a barbecue area. Additional conveniences include modern bathroom facilities and ample parking.

Area

The event is located in the Toggenburg area, nestled between the majestic Säntis and the striking Churfirsten peaksm and offers a diverse playground for outdoor enthusiasts. Visitors can hike along panoramic trails or bike through scenic landscapes. Unique attractions like the Klangweg trail and treetop adventures add an extra layer of fun and discovery.
